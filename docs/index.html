About Me

I'm a backend engineer drawn to the harder problems that surface when services stop trusting each other, databases diverge, or a single failure spreads further than it should. My work centers on distributed systems built with Go and Java, where good architecture mostly means anticipating what will eventually break.

That mindset carried into how I think about infrastructure. I treat cloud environments the same way I treat code: reproducible, version-controlled, and auditable. Working with AWS and Terraform pushed me to reason beyond the application layer and understand the full system I'm deploying into.

Right now I'm deepening my understanding of design patterns, software architectures, and cloud services, building a foundation that goes beyond getting things to work and toward understanding why they work.
